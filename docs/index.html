1. Information Collection & Use

QiCharge is designed with privacy-first principles. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII) or device identifiers.

All battery electrical telemetry (voltage, amperage, temperature, charging status) and device hardware metrics (RAM and internal storage capacity) are processed strictly in real-time on your local device. No data ever leaves your device.

2. Permissions Requested

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used exclusively to deliver user-configured charging alerts when your battery reaches the optimal 80%/85% threshold or when thermal safety limits are exceeded.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Used while charging to monitor live telemetry and trigger alarms when the screen is locked or the app is minimized.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to alert you via haptic vibration when your target charge percentage or temperature warning is reached.

3. Third-Party Services & Tracking

QiCharge does not incorporate third-party tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics frameworks that monitor your activity.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because no personal data is collected or transferred to external servers, we hold no user data to retain or delete.
